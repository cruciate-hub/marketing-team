# Template: playbook

**Use for:** vertical questions: how a specific kind of app uses social and community features. Examples: "How do fitness and health apps use community to retain users?", "Why do personal finance apps struggle with user retention?", "How do gaming and betting apps use social features?"

**Citations:** at least 1 external citation, usually about the vertical itself (category benchmarks, regulation, market data).

**Unique information:** at least one named example. Approved customers from `messaging/evidence-bank.md` where they fit the vertical; otherwise public, cited third-party examples. Never attribute a use case a customer hasn't publicly disclosed.

## Typical sections

- **Answer-first block** names what works in this vertical and why.
- **What makes this vertical different?** Usage patterns, constraints, regulation (health privacy, financial promotion rules, responsible gaming).
- **Which features work best?** Table: feature, what it does in this vertical, example, what to watch out for.
- **What results have apps seen?** Named examples with sourced numbers.
- **What are the pitfalls in this vertical?** Specific to the vertical, not generic.

## Rules

- Each vertical gets one playbook. A second page for the same vertical needs a genuinely different question (for example a diagnostic "why" question vs a "how they use" question) and must pass the duplication check.
- Vertical-specific rules and regulations are cited to primary sources and flagged for Legal review when they could be read as advice.
