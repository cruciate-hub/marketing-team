# Sound

Sound supports picture and voiceover; it never competes.

- **Explainers with a voiceover to come**: foley only, no music. Quiet (`S.master({gain: -8})`), each effect on the exact frame of its event: whoosh under whip pans and big camera moves, soft pops as items land, a low thud when something hits or slams, short highlighter "roll" on marks, a chime when the hero moment arrives, a swell into the end card and a ding on the underline.
- **Explainers without voiceover**: a light, warm score is fine (marimba or electric piano, soft kit), still under the foley.
- **Loops for GIF/WebP**: no sound.
- Never: risers, cinematic booms, vocal chops, existing songs or jingles.
