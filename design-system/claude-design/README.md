The design system of the social.plus website, as the live site has it on 7 October 2026: the 44 Webflow variables as tokens (names kept exactly, so `var(--social--main-blue)` in site CSS and here are the same thing), the foundations a page is made of, the 41 section types with their recipes, the taste guide on how to combine them, and the definition of done every page is checked against before it is called finished. Build pages only from these sections and foundations, with only these values. Nothing here is a team decision: every foundation and section is "draft" until Stefan or Amadeus approves it, "Known inconsistencies" is what the site does today, and "TO CHECK" means nobody decided yet. Neither is a rule to follow; say which you applied. Since 8 October 2026 the colours (role tokens and scales, dark and light) and the second font, Geist Mono for eyebrows and code, follow the social.plus web library (decisions by Amadeus) and are ahead of the live site; the live Webflow colour variables are kept under their own names so the captured sections still render as the site does.

## Content fundamentals

- Write the name exactly `social.plus`: lowercase, with the period, also at the start of a sentence and in title-case headings. Never Social.Plus, Social Plus, social plus or SocialPlus. (The live footer logo alt text still says "SocialPlus logo"; the X footer icon title still says "Social+": both known inconsistencies.)
- Facts about the product come from `messaging/product-capabilities.md` in the repository; the voice and terminology rules live in `messaging/` and are not part of this system.
- Headings on the site are short claims: "Save time with ready-to-use UIKits", "Build in-app messaging faster with a Chat SDK". A hero H1 is 5 to 9 words, its paragraph 25 to 45 words; the primary button is "Contact Sales".
- Uppercase only on tags and eyebrows (`superscript`, Geist Mono), with letter spacing. No text under 12px (`.tag` at 12.8px is the smallest live size; the blog grid's 10.8px tag is a known inconsistency).

## Color

- Use the role tokens (`color-*`) for everything new; never a scale step or a live variable directly. Dark is the default theme, light the second; the same markup works in both because only the role values change. The full table, the scales and the live-variable mapping are in the Colors section.
- Dark-first. `color-bg` is the page, `color-bg-alt` an alternate band, `color-surface` cards and panels, `color-surface-raised` menus, hover and inline code. Depth comes from these tonal layers, one step apart, with `color-border` one step above the layer it outlines, not from shadows.
- One blue for actions: `color-accent-fill` (blue-600 #3B41EC = `social--main-blue`), hover `color-accent-fill-hover` (blue-700), pressed `color-accent-fill-press` (blue-800), label `color-on-accent`. One primary per section.
- Blue-600 is never text on dark (2.84:1). Links, eyebrows and link-coloured icons use `color-link`: blue-400 #6F89FC on dark (5.99:1 on #111), blue-600 on light. Focus rings use `color-focus`.
- Text: `color-text` headings, `color-text-muted` body, `color-text-subtle` captions and meta (neutral-500 #939497 on dark, 6.23:1; it replaces the live grey-medium #717275, 3.93:1). Errors `color-error`.
- Scale steps for text: on light backgrounds 600 or darker (yellow 700), on dark 400 or lighter. The secondary hues decorate and signal status, never a call to action: green success, yellow warning (dark text on it), red error, purple, pink and orange decoration only.
- Gradients are backgrounds or shapes, never text: the blue `linear-gradient(145deg, var(--gradient-light-blue), var(--primary))` and the warm `linear-gradient(to right, var(--pink-300), var(--orange-400), var(--yellow-400))` (decoration and the logo mark only). The avatar ring is `linear-gradient(var(--yellow-400), var(--pink-300) 50%, var(--primary))`. The live `h1` radial text fill and the superscript gradient fill are known inconsistencies, not a licence.
- The captured sections use the live Webflow variables (`social--dark`, `text--text-color-grey-light`…). 19 of them are the same colour as a scale step; 17 keep their live value until the site is merged, each with its replacement named in its note. Do not use them for new work.
- A colour that is not a token is not used for new work. The ones regular sections depend on today (listed in the Colors section): #141414 radial gradient, #1b1b1b inputs, #dc3545 form errors (4.17:1, TO CHECK: `secondary--red`), #3ccb7f on #093a20 for `.tag.c-new`, #14152c industry hero, #2b2b2b icon holder.
- Emails render on white and follow the email spec (`emails/product-update-newsletter-spec.md`, font Inter): `main--whitesmoke` page, `main--white` content, `text--text-color-dark` headings, `text--text-color-grey-dark` body, `social--main-blue` buttons and links with white labels.

## Typography

- Two fonts. Figtree for everything, from `fonts/figtree.woff2` (social.plus's own variable build, weights 300 to 900, basic Latin only; the single-storey "a" is the default glyph). Never load Figtree from Google Fonts, the Webflow CDN or any other source, never add `font-feature-settings`. Stack `Figtree, Arial, sans-serif` (`--font--figtree` on the site, `figtree` here). Geist Mono for eyebrows and code only (code blocks, SDK snippets, API paths, token names), from `fonts/geist-mono.woff2` (variable 100 to 900; family `mono`, stack `"Geist Mono", ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, monospace`; decision 8 October 2026, not yet in Webflow). Emails are the exception (Inter).
- `html` 16px; everything is set in rem. The type scale is the web library's (decision 8 October 2026, not yet in Webflow): `body` 18px / 1.6, weight 400, `color-text-muted`, at most `measure-body` (65ch); `lead` for the intro paragraph under a hero or section heading (18 to 22px, `measure-lead` 52ch); `small` 16px for card body; `caption` 14px in `color-text-subtle` for meta and small print.
- Headings are weight 600, `color-text`. Sizes are fluid from a 360px to a 1440px screen through the Type scale tokens; the type styles are their 1440px specimens, so in CSS use the token (`font-size: var(--fluid-h2)`), never the specimen's fixed value:
  - `display` `fluid-display` 44px to 80px, leading 1.05, tracking -0.025em: the biggest hero statement only
  - `h1` `fluid-h1` 36px to 60px, leading 1.1, tracking -0.025em: one per page
  - `h2` `fluid-h2` 30px to 46px, leading 1.1, tracking -0.015em: section headings
  - `h3` `fluid-h3` 24px to 32px, leading 1.1, tracking -0.015em
  - `h4` `fluid-h4` 20px to 24px, leading 1.3: card and feature titles
  - `h5` 18px fixed, leading 1.3: small card titles, list headings and nav panel link titles (the live h6 maps here; the scale has no h6)
  Headings stop at `measure-title` (20ch; centred hero titles 16ch). Headline numbers are `stat-xl` (44 to 64px) and `stat-lg` (32 to 40px): weight 600, tracking -0.03em, `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`.
- The live site still renders its own scale (h1 and h2 both about 52px on desktop, body 17.6px): those styles are kept as `live-*` in the "Live site (until the merge)" group and the six live heading variables in Heading sizes and `components/bundle.css`, so the captured sections still render as the site does. Do not use them for new work.
- Eyebrows are `superscript` (Webflow class `.superscript`): Geist Mono 13px, 500, uppercase, letter spacing .08em, `color-link`; only when they add information. Code is `code` (15px) and `code-small` (13px) in Geist Mono. Button labels are `button-text` (16px, 500). Quotes are `blockquote` (24px, 600).
- Article bodies use the `rich-text-*` styles (headings 700, paragraphs 19.2px / 1.8); product updates and release notes the `product-update-*` styles. Every rich-text style undoes the h1 gradient fill; new work does not add one.
- Weights in use: 400 text, 500 buttons, links and the eyebrow, 600 headings and stat numbers, 700 rich-text headings (live). No others.

## Spacing and layout

- Spacing follows the web library (decision 8 October 2026, not yet in Webflow): nine steps on a 4px grid, `space-3xs` 4px, `space-2xs` 8px, `space-xs` 12px, `space-s` 16px, `space-m` 24px, `space-l` 32px, `space-xl` 48px, `space-2xl` 64px, `space-3xl` 96px. Every gap, margin and padding is one of them. They name the values the live stylesheet already uses most (1rem, 1.5rem, .5rem, 2rem, .75rem).
- Section rhythm (Layout tokens, fluid from 360 to 1440px): `section-pad-m` 64 to 112px top and bottom is the default; `section-pad-s` 48 to 80px for logo walls, bands and thin sections; `section-pad-l` 80 to 144px for heroes and the closing CTA. The page gutter is `gutter` (20 to 40px); grids that are not frame cells use `grid-gap` (16 to 32px).
- Containers: `container-l` 1280px by default, `container-m` 1024px for pricing, tables and text-heavy sections, `container-s` 768px for centred heroes, FAQ and articles. The frame lines sit on the container edges: the 12-column grid, the column inset, frame lines, cells, plus markers, hatched bands and the art panel are in the Grid and frame section and the Frame card (decision 8 October 2026). They are the target for new sections; the captured live sections do not use them, and the "Building a page" rule switches to the frame when the web library's framed sections join the set.
- Breakpoints are Webflow's defaults, desktop first: 992px and up, 768 to 991px, 480 to 767px, below 480px.
- None of these is a Webflow variable: in a Webflow build write the value (or the clamp) each token's note gives. The live tokens (`spacing-xs` to `spacing-xl`, `section-padding-y` 80px, `page-padding`, `grid-2-gap` 4rem, `container-large` to `container-small`) are kept so the captured sections can be read against them; they are not for new work. The live 480px `container-small` (forms) has no library equivalent yet.
- Mobile: text stacks above media, nothing scrolls sideways at 390px, the 72px icon holder becomes 48px; section padding and gutter shrink on their own.

## Radius, shadows and borders

- Radius follows the web library (decision 8 October 2026, not yet in Webflow; it is the reduced set the site audit proposed): `radius-s` 8px for icon tiles, inputs and small controls, `radius-m` 16px for free-standing cards, thumbnails and images, `radius-l` 24px for panels and large visuals, `radius-pill` (999px) for buttons, badges, tags, tabs and chips, `radius-circle` for avatars and dots. Frame cells have no radius. None is a Webflow variable: write the value. The live radii stay as tokens for reading the captured sections (`radius-4` to `radius-24`, `radius-tag`, `live-radius-pill`, `live-radius-circle`), each naming its replacement; they are not for new work.
- Depth comes from tonal surfaces (`color-bg`, `color-bg-alt`, `color-surface`, `color-surface-raised`) and `color-border`, not shadows. The one shadow is `shadow-float`, for floating layers only (menus, dropdowns, popovers, the nav sheet). No glow, no card shadow in new work; the live shadows (`shadow-card` to `shadow-focus-field`) are kept for reading the captured sections.
- Borders: `border--border-dark` dividers and card borders, `border--border-hover` on hover and on the nav and sub-nav, `border--border-dark-grey` the secondary button outline (3.29:1, passes as a control border), `border--border-med-grey` and `border--border-light-grey` on light surfaces. The style guide `.divider` is `border--border-light-grey` even on dark pages (known inconsistency; dark pages use `border--border-dark` for the same job).
- Cards in one set are greyscale (the dark surfaces with a `border--border-dark` border); colour or a gradient goes on one highlighted card at most.

## Iconography

- Interface icons are inline SVG with `fill="currentColor"`, so they take the text colour: the arrow (every button, `cta-button_icon-size`; 1rem small on text links), the checkmark (1rem, `social--main-blue`), the external-link glyph (1rem, the one stroked outline), the FAQ chevron (1.1rem), the info mark (1.15rem), nav glyphs (1rem), the footer social glyphs (1.5rem, `border--border-med-grey` value, `social--main-blue` on hover). No icon font; no other sizes.
- Feature glyphs are SVG files in Webflow assets inside one of two holders: white filled shapes in the `card-icon-v1-size` blue circle, blue-gradient filled shapes in the `card-icon-v2-size` grey square. A new glyph follows the look of its holder; do not draw a new icon style for a page, propose it.
- Blue is the only icon accent, as a holder, never as a glyph colour on dark. Decorative icons carry `aria-hidden="true"`; an icon that is a link's only content carries a `<title>` or the link an `aria-label`. Pair icons with text in body copy.
- The interface glyphs are inline SVG in the markup (the Icons preview shows them as the site renders them); every feature glyph and logo file the sections show is in `assets/media/`, copied as served from the site.

## Logo

Use the files, never a low-resolution copy or a redrawn mark. The white and black mono sets are not in the repository (TO CHECK: Figma or the brand kit).
- On the website (dark pages): use the full logo in colour with the white wordmark, the file `66fe6169153dc88a03557da6_29a5aaff9e31043f2175762ac854a9e5_logo.svg` in the `media` asset group, as the Nav (G1) and Footer (G5) cards show it. Use it as it is; never the #111 wordmark on a dark page. A logo that renders black is a bug: the file lost its colours (SVG colours must sit on the shapes, not in a `<style>` block, because uploaded SVGs lose their style blocks).
- The mark: the warm arm (`secondary--pink` to #f66005 to `secondary--yellow`) and the blue arm (`gradient--medium-blue` to `social--main-blue` to `gradient--light-blue`) meet in a navy centre (`social--button-pressed`). Its gradient stops are artwork, not tokens.
- Third-party logos appear only from the approved set in the `third-party` asset group (today `ai-tools/`: Claude, also used for Claude Code; Cursor; VS Code; GitHub Copilot, labelled "Copilot" on the site; OpenAI, for Codex and ChatGPT) or from files the team supplies; a tool or company without an approved file is written as text. They are single-colour marks in `text--text-color-grey-light`, shown in the 40px "Works in" tile of Hero / Simple. Never redraw, recolour beyond that grey, crop, or set one in our colours. Adding one: the official single-colour mark, set to #b3b3b3 on the shapes, no `<style>` block, approved by Stefan or Amadeus.
- Clear space the height of the mark on all sides; minimum size TO CHECK (do not go below the 2.5rem nav box). Never on `social--main-blue`. Never stretch, rotate, recolour, add a shadow or a glow. Co-branding: full logo next to full logo or mark next to mark, equal weight, clear separation.

## Imagery

- Two modes, never mixed on one layout. Product illustrations (feature sections, product pages, documentation, empty states): 3D object-based, UI-metaphor driven, one concept per image, dark background (`social--dark`, `social--dark-gray-background`, `social--grey`), blue the only accent, one soft blue glow, radius 16 to 24px, no human figures, no pink, orange or yellow. Photography (campaign heroes, social, events): people in real collaboration, dark and high-contrast, cool slightly desaturated grade, a dark overlay rgba(17,17,17,0.5 to 0.7) under text.
- Blog headers, answer images and glossary images are the one exception: a dark interface scene with a blue glow and one real element (a cut-out person, a hand with a phone, faces in avatar slots, a group photo, or official logos in comparisons); no readable text; built as editable vectors in the shared Figma file and exported WebP at 1578 × 888 (thumbnails 724 × 408 and 502 × 283), answers 1240 × 840.
- Ratio classes `.image-16-9`, `.image-2-1`, `.image-3-1`, `.image-3-2` carry `radius-16`; `.image-4-3` has none (known inconsistency). Decorative visuals (blurred brand-blue or warm shapes at 5 to 15% opacity, fine grain on #111) always sit behind content.

## Accessibility floor

- WCAG 2.1 AA for every page, email and graphic: 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for large text (24px, or 19px bold), control borders, icons and focus rings. Every text token's usage note names the grounds it reads on with the ratio; the full pair table is in the Accessibility section. A live pair that fails stays as the site has it and is marked TO CHECK, never quietly re-tinted.
- 44px is the target for every interactive element (`button-min-height` is 44.8px, inputs `input-height` 52px); 12px is the text floor. Focus: links `outline: .125rem solid` in the text colour with `.125rem` offset; text fields `shadow-focus-field`; never `outline: none` without a replacement; use `:focus-visible`.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`; the captured sections show the still state, which is also the reduced-motion state to build. Accordions, tabs and sliders work with the keyboard or the page uses the static state; headings in order, one `<h1>`, card titles `<h3>`; meaningful images get alt text, decorative ones `alt=""`; forms have visible labels and text errors linked to the field.

## Building a page

- Build pages only from the section set (the Components view, the groups named "Section · …": Global, Heroes, Content, Listings, Articles, vs, Pricing; the Foundations group above them holds the building blocks) and the foundations, each as written there: its structure, its content slots, only its allowed variations, and its inner layout as the captured preview shows it (in the card grids the icon sits top-left above the title, never beside the text; a different inner layout is a proposal). Follow the page recipes (product page, product landing, SDK page, industry page, vs, blog post, answer, glossary entry, customer story) for the section order; a product or feature that is not a family of its own (Agentry, the MCP server, Vise, an add-on) is a product landing page. Use only these tokens, Figtree from the file, `social--dark` as the ground, `social--main-blue` as the only action colour.
- Combine the sections as the Taste section says: calm pages with one focal point per screen, dense and airy sections alternating, one primary action per section and per screen region, real product visuals (never a drawn diagram in the product's place), blue as the one accent, headings that are claims, generous spacing from the steps, no one-off effects, dark first, built for 1440 and checked at 390, numbers either real and sourced or labelled "Illustrative example". Apply it when you build and again when you check the rendered page.
- Do not invent a new section, text effect, icon style, shadow or colour. If a page needs something the set does not have, stop and propose it: name the section, say what it is for, show one example, mark it "proposed" on the page itself and in the reply. It may be used only after Stefan or Amadeus approves it; then it joins the set. Until then build on the closest set section and say so. The proposals waiting today are in the Proposed sections section (use-case tabs: until approved, question-and-answer use cases are built on Accordion with image, 17, and the reply says "built on 17; use-case tabs proposed").
- Each section component's guidelines say when to use it, when not, its content slots with live examples, what is allowed and not, and its accessibility and mobile behaviour; its preview is the live markup and CSS captured from the site in one static state (tabs, sliders, marquees and accordions show one state). Class names come from Webflow and are needed by the copied CSS.
- Changes to the system are approved by Stefan or Amadeus; the site is captured again after a site change (`design-system/capture/` in the repository), then this system is re-synced.

## Definition of done

Before saying a page is finished, check each point and report it in the reply. "Looks good" is not a check.

1. The page follows a recipe in the Page recipes section; if none fits, the reply names the closest recipe and where and why the page departs from it.
2. The global nav (G1) and the footer (G5) are on the page, and the footer CTA band (G4) where the recipe has it.
3. Every section is one from the set, used as its guidelines allow, with its inner layout as captured (where the icon, image and text sit, their alignment and order; only the content changes). Anything else is marked "proposed" on the page itself and in the reply.
4. Only values from the tokens, colours through the role tokens; Figtree from `fonts/figtree.woff2`, Geist Mono from `fonts/geist-mono.woff2` for eyebrows and code only; no new text effect, icon style, shadow or colour. A token that is not a Webflow variable (the role and scale colours until the merge, and the Type scale, Spacing, Layout, Radius, Shadows, Container, Size and Heading sizes sections) is written as its literal value or class in a Webflow build, never as `var(--<token>)`.
5. One primary (blue) button per section; two actions of equal weight only with approval.
6. Example or illustrative numbers, charts and sample answers carry the visible label "Illustrative example"; real numbers carry their source.
7. Third-party logos only from the approved set in the `third-party` asset group (listed in the Logo section, "Third-party logos") or files supplied by the team; a tool or company without an approved file is written as text.
8. Links that do not exist yet are clear placeholders (`href="#"` and a note of the intended path), never invented URLs.
9. Accessibility basics: one `<h1>`, headings in order, alt text on meaningful images, token pairs with AA contrast, interactive parts (tabs, accordions) are real buttons that work with the keyboard, or the static state is used.
10. Render the page at 1440 and 390, check it against the design system and the Taste section, and list every mismatch in the reply ("none found" counts as a check).
