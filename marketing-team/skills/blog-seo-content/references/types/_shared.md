# Shared rules for every blog type

Every type template fills this frame. The template decides structure, evidence and length; the frame never changes.

**Metadata block** (labeled lines under the H1, in this order): Meta description, Slug, Alt text, Category, Tags, Minutes to read, Type, Queue ID, Author (required for opinion and original-research), Last updated (required when the title carries a year), Editor (named human reviewer).

**Intro paragraph** (maps to `post-summary`): 1-3 sentences, contains the target keyword, never restates the title. For blog posts the intro can hook rather than answer, but it must still say what the reader will get.

**Body:** H2 every 200-300 words; H3 for sub-points. Headings may be questions where that reads naturally, but it is not required on the blog.

**Evidence:** every claim about social.plus or a customer comes from an `Approved` row in `messaging/evidence-bank.md`. Every statistic has a source. Information-gain bar from SKILL.md applies to every type.

**Close:** a closing section that gives a next step or a decision rule, not a recap. The CTA links to the most relevant social.plus page (chosen by `internal-linking-strategist`, never improvised).

**Boundaries:** if the post's core is "How do you [do X]?" for a buyer or developer, it belongs to aeo-content; if it's "What is [term]?", it belongs to glossary-content. Product education (how to use a social.plus feature) is the one how-to that stays on the blog.
