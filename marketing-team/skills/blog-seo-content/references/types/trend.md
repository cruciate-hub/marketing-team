# Type: trend

**For:** what changed in the market and what it means: platform shifts, regulation, new user behaviour.

**Length:** 900-1,800 words. **Required:** at least 3 sourced statistics.

## Structure

1. **Intro:** the change, stated as a fact with a date and source.
2. **What changed** (H2): the evidence, dated.
3. **Why it's happening** (H2).
4. **What it means for product teams** (H2): concrete implications.
5. **What to watch next** (H2): signals that would confirm or reverse the trend.

## Rules

- Every trend claim has a dated source. "Everyone is talking about X" is not a trend.
- A year in the title only when the content is genuinely year-specific; add `Last updated:` and a refresh owner. Compliance WARNs on year-in-title otherwise.
- Don't dress a product announcement as a trend.
