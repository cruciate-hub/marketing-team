What social.plus delivers beyond the community platform

Social and community layer built inside your app

Circle creates a standalone community site, a place members visit separately. social.plus activity feeds, chat, communities, and events integrate as components inside your existing product, where your users already are. You keep your app as the destination and add the social layer to it, rather than sending users somewhere else. Your brand retains full control of the experience, the data, and the user journey.

Full-stack SDKs with complete white-label customization

Built for fast and stable integration, social.plus offers pre-built SDK and UIKit components for iOS, Android, Flutter, React Native, and Web, so your brand can ship social experiences without starting from scratch. Every component is fully customizable from colors to complete white-label control. Circle Headless offers a REST API that requires custom front-end development on every surface, which suits teams wanting total control over their UI.

Monetization built into every surface

Every part of your app becomes a storefront with social.plus. Product catalogues, cross-surface tagging, shoppable livestreams, and sponsored content capture high-intent signals right where users are already engaged. Circle monetizes through paid memberships, courses, and gated content, with revenue tied to those specific areas and a per-transaction fee on every plan.

AI-powered analytics and insights on data you fully own

social.plus provides real-time, AI-powered analytics on what drives engagement, growth, and retention. Agentry lets teams query their data in plain language inside Claude, ChatGPT, and other AI assistants, and your brand owns every data point. Circle keeps analytics in its own ecosystem, covering high-level engagement and relying on integrations for custom reporting, plus an MCP server for plain-language queries.

Social engagement infrastructure that scales with you

social.plus offers modular components so your team can start with what you need and grow from there. Powering global brands with millions of MAUs, the infrastructure scales as your user base expands. It also includes an operational layer for every stakeholder running live community experiences. Circle excels for creator-led or mid-sized communities, but scaling to high volumes can bring significant cost and overhead.
