# Shared page shape (all four templates)

Every template below fills this shape. The template decides what goes in the H2s and which table to use; the shape itself never changes.

1. **Title (H1)** = the Queue row's canonical question, word for word.
2. **Metadata block** (labeled lines): Meta description, Slug, Alt text, Intent, Queue ID, Last updated, Editor (named human reviewer).
3. **Answer-first block** (no heading): 1-2 sentences, 25-60 words. Sentence 1 answers the title directly and contains its keyword phrase. No mention of social.plus.
4. **Summary paragraph** (no heading): 80-150 words that stand alone if lifted out of the page. No mention of social.plus.
5. **Body H2s** = the Queue row's sub-questions, phrased as questions (at least 60% of body H2s must end in "?"). Each section is self-contained: a reader landing mid-page understands it. H3s are allowed inside a section.
6. **At least one table**, placed where it answers a sub-question.
7. **Pitch section**: exactly one H2 that names social.plus, 80-150 words. Heading written for this page, for example "How social.plus helps teams launch in-app events". Never "Leading [X] for [Y]: social.plus" or "Why social.plus powers...". Built from `messaging/positioning.md`, `value-story.md` and `boilerplates.md`, tied to this specific question. Customer facts only from `messaging/evidence-bank.md`.
8. **FAQs**: H2 "FAQs" with 3-5 H3 questions from real research (Ahrefs PAA, search suggestions, sales/support questions). Answers start directly and are 1-3 sentences. An FAQ that fully answers another Queue row's question is replaced by a sentence linking to that page. No mention of social.plus.
9. **Conclusion**: H2 "Conclusion", 2-4 sentences ending in a decision rule or next step. Never opens with "In conclusion". No links.

Length: 700-1,500 words. Pages that answer one question well beat pages that cover a whole topic.
