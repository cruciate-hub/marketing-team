# Type: opinion

**For:** a stance the team actually holds, argued with evidence. Examples: "Why community is emerging as a core GTM channel", "The future of community is ambient".

**Length:** 900-1,800 words. **Required:** `Author:` naming the person whose view this is (compliance FAILs without it). A placeholder is allowed in the draft and must be replaced before publish.

## Structure

1. **Intro:** the claim in one sentence, and why it matters now.
2. **The claim, stated plainly** (H2): what we believe and what the common view gets wrong.
3. **The evidence** (1-3 H2s): data from the evidence bank, cited research, named examples. Every section adds proof, not repetition.
4. **The strongest counter-argument** (H2): state it fairly and answer it. Opinion pieces that ignore the obvious objection read as marketing.
5. **What to do about it** (H2): 2-4 concrete implications for the reader.

## Rules

- One claim per post. If there are two, there are two posts.
- The social.plus angle is at most one section and never the argument itself.
- Brand narrative (`narrative.md`: context, tension, infrastructure, impact, advantage) fits this type best; use it as the spine where it helps.
