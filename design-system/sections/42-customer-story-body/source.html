Activerse is a dynamic development company dedicated to building innovative health and wellness apps for personal trainers, influencers, and fitness professionals. With a strong focus on engagement and community-driven experiences, they help their clients create digital platforms that go beyond just workouts and meal plans—they foster real connections between users.

The Challenge

Building community at scale

As Activerse continued to grow, they faced a critical challenge: providing interactive community features at scale across multiple client apps. They recognized that social interaction is a key driver of motivation in health and wellness, whether it's users encouraging each other, discussing progress, or engaging with their favorite fitness influencers. However, building and maintaining these social features in-house was complex and resource-intensive.

Additionally, Activerse wanted to leverage existing Infrastructure-as-a-Service solutions rather than build everything from scratch. They needed a flexible, scalable way to integrate social capabilities seamlessly into various apps, ensuring that each platform felt unique while maintaining a high level of user engagement.

Why social.plus

A flexible & scalable solution

After evaluating different options, Activerse partnered with social.plus to bring a seamless social experience to their clients' apps. social.plus stood out for its flexibility, allowing Activerse to integrate social, chat, and video features directly into apps with minimal friction. This meant that fitness influencers and personal trainers could interact directly with their users, while community-driven apps could facilitate peer-to-peer motivation and engagement.

More importantly, social.plus provided room for growth—Activerse could start with core social features and expand into chat and video as needed, depending on each client's requirements. This adaptability made it the perfect long-term solution for their expanding ecosystem of fitness and wellness apps.

Implementation and Use Case

Bringing social features to life

Activerse's first implementation with social.plus was for Diet by Ann, a nutrition and workout training app created by renowned fitness expert Anna Lewandowska. They began by rolling out discussion feeds and dedicated group spaces, allowing users to talk about topics like nutrition, fitness routines, and wellness tips. These features created a structured environment where users could engage, share progress, and support one another, making the app experience more interactive and community-driven.

By seamlessly embedding these features into Diet by Ann, Activerse was able to transform the app from a simple fitness tool into an engaging social hub where users stayed active not just for workouts, but for conversations, advice, and motivation.

Results and Impact

Driving engagement and growth

The addition of social features proved to be a game-changer. With over 45,000+ active users and steady month-over-month growth, Diet by Ann became more than just a fitness and nutrition app—it became a thriving community. Activerse saw firsthand how discussion capabilities drove deeper engagement, making users more likely to return to the app and participate in conversations.

This successful implementation reinforced Activerse's belief in the power of social interaction within the health and wellness space. The ability to connect users with one another, as well as with fitness influencers, created an environment where motivation and accountability flourished.

Future Plans

Expanding social capabilities across more apps

Looking ahead, Activerse is excited to bring these social capabilities to more client apps, using Diet by Ann as a successful blueprint. With the growing demand for interactive fitness experiences, they plan to expand beyond discussion feeds into chat and video functionalities, tailoring each implementation to the specific needs of their clients.

By continuing to leverage social.plus, Activerse is set to redefine how fitness and wellness apps engage users—transforming them from solo workout tools into vibrant, supportive communities that keep people coming back for more.
