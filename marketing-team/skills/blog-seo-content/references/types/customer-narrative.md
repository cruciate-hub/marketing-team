# Type: customer-narrative

**For:** lessons from named customers, told as a story with a point: "What we learned launching communities with fitness apps". Not the case study itself; that belongs to `case-study`.

**Length:** 900-1,800 words. **Required:** at least one approved customer from `messaging/evidence-bank.md` (compliance FAILs otherwise).

## Structure

1. **Intro:** the lesson in one sentence.
2. **The situation** (H2): the customer's context and problem, from public or approved material.
3. **What they did** (H2).
4. **What happened** (H2): approved figures only.
5. **The lesson for other teams** (H2): what transfers and what doesn't.

## Rules

- Never attribute a use case or figure a customer hasn't publicly disclosed or approved.
- Link the customer's first mention to their customer story (via `internal-linking-strategist`).
- Check with `case-study` that this doesn't duplicate an existing story.
