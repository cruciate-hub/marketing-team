# Template: explainer

**Use for:** "why", "does", "what is a good..." questions where the reader wants to understand a cause, an effect or a benchmark. Examples: "Why does app retention drop after the first week?", "Do social features improve user retention?", "What is a good retention rate for a mobile app?"

Not for "What is [term]?" definitions. Those belong to `glossary-content`; link to the glossary entry instead.

**Citations:** at least 2 external citations. Claims about why something happens, or whether something works, need evidence beyond social.plus's own marketing.

**Unique information:** this is the template where original data matters most. Aim for a platform benchmark or approved range from `messaging/evidence-bank.md`, a cited study, or a worked example with real numbers. If the page's core claim can only be supported by pending platform data, the Queue row stays at `Blocked: needs data`.

## Typical sections

- **Answer-first block** states the answer and its main reason: "Retention drops after week one because..."
- **What does the evidence show?** The data, with sources. A table works well here: benchmark by category, cohort comparison, or cause vs symptom.
- **Why does it happen?** The mechanism, in plain language.
- **When doesn't it apply?** Exceptions and limits. Explainers that admit limits are more credible and more citable.
- **What should you do about it?** Two or three practical implications, each linking to the relevant how-to page where one exists.

## Rules

- Separate what the data shows from what you infer. Mark inference as inference.
- Benchmarks always carry their source, sample and date.
- Don't answer a neighbouring Queue row's question in full. Link to it.
