# Content and locale

What appears as readable content inside an illustration. The audience and the brand voice are American; placeholder content should feel like a US consumer app.

## What may be readable

Only what makes the image understandable: a person's name, a significant button label, a price, a short status ("Silenced", "Liked by Emily & friends"), up to ~4 low-contrast labels in a background pattern, and words the brief itself is about (a headline on a social post, a number the source quotes). Everything else is grey bars.

## Placeholder people

- **American first names**, common and diverse: Emily, Jordan, Marcus, Ava, Tyler, Sofia, Ethan, Chloe, Malik, Hannah, Jayden, Olivia, Mia, Andre, Priya, Kevin, Grace, Luis, Nicole, Caleb.
- **Surnames when needed**: Carter, Brooks, Rivera, Nguyen, Patel, Johnson, Kim, Walker, Garcia, Thompson, Lee, Bennett.
- **Avoid** Scandinavian/Nordic names and spellings (no Lindqvist, Ek, Jonas, Ingrid, å/ä/ö), and real public figures.
- Mix genders and backgrounds across an image and across posts; don't reuse the last image's name.
- Names are fictional; say so when delivering.

## Conventions

- **Currency**: US dollars, `$79`, `$1,299` (never €, kr, or comma decimals).
- **Spelling in UI copy**: US English (color, favorite, canceled).
- **Dates and times**: `Oct 14`, `2:30 PM`, `10/14` — never 24-hour or day-first.
- **Numbers**: comma thousands (`12,400`), period decimals.
- **Products**: US-mainstream goods (sneakers, headphones, a water bottle, a hoodie), no region-specific brands.
- **Brand**: always `social.plus` (lowercase, with the period).

## Facts

- No invented metrics, counters, quotes, testimonials or approvals presented as real.
- A number may appear only when the source (post, page, brief) quotes it; keep its exact value and, in editorial pieces, add a source line.
- Counts on reactions, members, likes are bars, not numbers.
- Other companies and technologies the content is clearly about **do** appear, as their official logos — see `third-party-logos.md` (decide from the whole article, official files only, never drawn). Never show another company's product UI.
