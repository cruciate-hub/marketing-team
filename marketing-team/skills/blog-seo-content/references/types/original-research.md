# Type: original-research

**For:** findings from social.plus platform data, surveys or analysis no one else can publish. The single highest-value content the engine can produce, for readers, for AI engines and for earned media.

**Length:** 1,200-2,500 words. **Required:** `Author:`, a methodology heading, at least one data table, at least 5 statistics (compliance FAILs otherwise).

**Gate:** every figure comes from an `Approved` evidence-bank row. Platform data needs Legal approval of both the metric and the wording; until then the Queue row stays `Blocked: needs data`.

## Structure

1. **Intro:** the headline finding with its number.
2. **Key findings** (H2): 3-5 findings, each one sentence with a figure. This is the block other sites and AI engines quote.
3. **One H2 per finding:** the data (table or chart description), what it means, and what it doesn't show.
4. **Methodology** (H2, e.g. "How we measured this"): data source, sample size, period, definitions, anonymisation, known limits.
5. **What teams should do with this** (H2).

## Rules

- Separate findings from interpretation. Mark inference as inference.
- Never round up, cherry-pick periods or combine metrics into a new claim.
- Plan a refresh date; research posts are the ones worth updating yearly.
