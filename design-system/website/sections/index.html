Foundations

Accordion row

The FAQ row (`.accordion_item`) and the feature accordion item (`.add-ons_accordion-item`) as single rows, open. · preview.html · foundation.md

Buttons

Primary, secondary and text link from the style guide, plus the form submit button. States are shown statically: the live `:hover` and `:active` rules are copied onto `.sim-hover` and `.sim-active` classes in the preview. · preview.html · foundation.md

Cards

The four card styles as they are today (v1, v2, v3, plain), the thumbnail card of every listing and the customer story card. · preview.html · foundation.md

Colors

The 44 live Webflow variables (General and Typography collections) as swatches with name, value and use. The page is dark-first: `--social--dark` #111 is the page background and `--social--main-blue` #3B41EC is the only action colour. · preview.html · foundation.md

Dividers

The `.divider` line and the text divider of the logo wall. · preview.html · foundation.md

Form inputs

The contact form (text fields, selects, textarea, custom checkbox, submit) and the footer newsletter form, with the site's own markup and rules. Success and error states are not captured. · preview.html · foundation.md

Image ratios

The image ratio classes of the style guide. · preview.html · foundation.md

Rich text (article body)

The rich-text styles as they are today: `.rich-text` (blog with `.c-blog`, glossary, tutorials, news, legal with `.c-legal`), `.answers_rich-text`, `.product-update_rich-text` (product updates, release notes) and the plain `.w-richtext`. Long bodies are trimmed in the preview to the first elements of each kind. · preview.html · foundation.md

Spacing and containers

The five named steps, the four containers, and the section rhythm and page gutter every page relies on. · preview.html · foundation.md

Tags

The `.tag` pill and its variants: default, `.c-dark`, `.c-new`, `.c-nav-webinar`. · preview.html · foundation.md

Typography

Figtree (variable font 300 to 900; Inter is loaded but unused). `html` 16px; `body` 1.1rem = 17.6px / 1.6 on `--text--text-color-grey-light`. Heading sizes live on six typography tokens; the preview measures every sample at 1440 and 390 px. · preview.html · foundation.md
