Touch targets and text sizes

Interactive elements need 44px (WCAG 2.5.8 says 24px minimum; the brand keeps 44px). The primary button is 2.8rem = 44.8px high; text links and footer links are a text line (about 22px) and need padding or spacing (TO CHECK). Smallest text on the site: tags .8rem = 12.8px; the blog grid overrides the tag to 10.8px, below the 12px floor (TO CHECK).

Motion

The brand rule: every animation and transition respects prefers-reduced-motion; no flashing above 3 per second. On the live site only the nav and the /vs/ hero carry a reduced-motion rule; the logo marquee, the count-up numbers and the GSAP reveals keep moving (TO CHECK). Section captures show the still state.
